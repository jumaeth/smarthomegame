### Bio
Lina is a curious and creative 14-year-old student from Aarau. She enjoys school, especially when lessons involve hands-on activities or digital tools. Outside of class, Lina spends a lot of time online — whether it’s watching tech videos, chatting with friends, or trying out new apps and games.
She’s really into smart technology and often experiments with her family's Alexa device. She likes setting up routines or finding out how voice assistants work. She dreams of having her own smart home setup one day. For Lina, learning and playing often go hand in hand — she likes when a game teaches her something new without making it feel like studying.
### Interests
• Technology and how things work

• Playing games online with friends

### Needs and expectations
• An interesting, nice pixel game with cool tech elements
• Challenges and quizzes to have fun and learn something new
• Something she can talk about or play with friends
• A learning experience that doesn’t feel like school
• Smooth user experience on tablet or computer
### Pain Points
• Loses interest if the game feels too simple or outdated
• Gets annoyed when adults explain tech like she doesn’t get it
• Doesn't enjoy games that are too slow or overly educational
• Doesn't know anything about internet security
• Doesn't look in the terms & conditions
### How She Interacts with the Software
Lina discovers the SmarHomeEscape game during a lesson at school, where it’s introduced as part of the educational process. Although she usually likes playing games secretly during class, this time she’s excited that playing is actually allowed — and even encouraged. The topic of smart devices and internet safety immediately grabs her interest, especially since she’s already familiar with tools like Alexa at home. She’s curious to explore how the game presents these concepts and enjoys solving challenges with her classmates. For Lina, it’s a great change from typical lessons — learning through a game makes the topic feel more real and fun. She looks forward to playing the game whenever it's part of class and likes discussing it with her friends during and after the session.
