### Bio

Margit is juggles work and home life, always making time for her child despite her busy schedule. She knows kids love games and doesn’t mind hers playing them as long as it’s safe, age-appropriate, and not just mindless screen time.

She likes to research what her child is playing, often checking parent forums or seeking advice from teachers, before giving the green light. She doesn’t mind fun games but prefers ones that teach something useful, like problem-solving or creativity.

### Interests
Margit is interessted in technology, IoT and smart homes. She likes to connect new devices to her home and automate everyday tasks.

### Needs and expectations

* A game that balances fun and learning.  
* Easy to get insight into the game to determine if the game content is age appropriate. 
* Wants to understand the game herself so she can discuss it with her child.  
* No subscription charged for the game, it should be free.
* The game should effectively convey learning objectives.
